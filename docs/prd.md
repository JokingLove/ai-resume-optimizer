# AI Resume Optimizer Product Requirements Document

## 1. User Personas

### Primary Users
- **Job Seekers**: Recent graduates, mid-career professionals, and senior individuals actively applying for jobs.
- **Career Changers**: Professionals looking to transition to a new industry or role, needing to reframe their experience.
- **Freelancers/Contractors**: Individuals seeking gig work who need to quickly tailor resumes for specific projects.

### Secondary Users
- **Career Coaches**: Professionals who assist clients in job search and want to leverage AI tools for better outcomes.
- **University Career Centers**: Institutions providing job search resources to students and alumni.

## 2. Problem Statement

- **Low Resume Passing Rate**: Many resumes fail to pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) due to poor keyword optimization and formatting issues.
- **Poor Job Description Match**: Candidates struggle to tailor their resumes effectively for each job application, leading to low interview rates.
- **Time-Consuming Manual Process**: Customizing resumes for each application is labor-intensive and inconsistent.
- **Lack of Actionable Feedback**: Traditional resume builders offer generic advice without specific, data-driven insights for improvement.

## 3. Solution Overview

An AI-powered resume optimization tool that analyzes job descriptions and user resumes to provide real-time, tailored suggestions for improving ATS compatibility and relevance. The tool will:
- Parse and extract key requirements from job descriptions.
- Compare resume content against job descriptions to identify gaps.
- Offer specific edits (keyword additions, rephrasing, formatting) to increase match scores.
- Provide a match score and actionable insights.
- Optionally generate tailored resume versions for different applications.

## 4. Core Features

### 4.1 Resume Analysis & Scoring
- ATS compatibility scoring (format, keywords, sections).
- Job description match percentage analysis.
- Skill gap identification.

### 4.2 Optimization Suggestions
- Keyword recommendations based on job description.
- Phrasing improvements for bullet points and summary.
- Formatting tips (sections, file type, length).
- Action verbs and quantifiable achievement suggestions.

### 4.3 Tailoring Engine
- Ability to upload or paste a job description.
- Generate a customized resume version highlighting relevant experience.
- Option to accept/reject individual suggestions.
- Version history for different job applications.

### 4.4 User Experience
- Simple upload/paste interface for resume and job description.
- Real-time scoring and suggestions as user edits.
- Export options (PDF, DOCX, plain text).
- Integration with popular job boards (LinkedIn, Indeed) via browser extension (future).

### 4.5 AI Models
- NLP models for job description parsing and skill extraction.
- Matching algorithms (TF-IDF, BERT-based semantic similarity).
- Generative AI for rewriting suggestions (optional, with user control).

## 5. Success Metrics

### 5.1 Acquisition & Activation
- Number of registered users.
- Percentage of users who upload a resume and job description.
- Activation rate (users who receive at least one suggestion).

### 5.2 Engagement
- Average time spent per optimization session.
- Number of suggestions accepted per session.
- Repeat usage rate (users returning for multiple job applications).

### 5.3 Outcome Metrics
- Self-reported increase in interview requests (via surveys).
- ATS score improvement before/after optimization.
- User satisfaction (NPS, CSAT).

### 5.4 Business Metrics
- Conversion rate from free to paid (if freemium model).
- Churn rate.
- Revenue per user (if subscription).
